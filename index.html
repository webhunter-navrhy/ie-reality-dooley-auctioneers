Description

Dooley Auctioneers are delighted to present 8 The Poplars, Delgany to the market, a charming detached two-bedroom bungalow extending to approximately 87 sq. m. (936 sq. ft.), standing on a generous site of approximately 0.22 acre, in one of North Wicklow's most sought-after residential locations.

Detached bungalows on sites of this size are rarely offered to the market, and No. 8 presents an exciting opportunity for purchasers seeking a comfortable single-storey home in a peaceful village setting, while also offering excellent scope for future enhancement, subject to the necessary planning permissions.

The property is presented in good condition throughout and benefits from a newly fitted kitchen and a bright, practical layout. The accommodation comprises a welcoming entrance hallway, two well-proportioned bedrooms, a family bathroom and an impressive open-plan kitchen/dining/living area which forms the heart of the home. This spacious room provides an excellent setting for both everyday living and entertaining, with a strong connection to the surrounding gardens.

A particular feature of No. 8 is the superb wraparound garden, extending across this generous 0.22-acre site. The rear garden enjoys a highly desirable west/southwest-facing aspect, providing excellent afternoon and evening sunshine. A sunny patio creates the perfect setting for al fresco dining, entertaining or simply relaxing, while the mature surroundings provide an attractive sense of privacy and tranquillity.

The property also benefits from interesting previous planning history. The site previously benefited from planning permission for a new detached dwelling under Planning Reference 23/60473. While any future development would be subject to a new planning application and the requirements of Wicklow County Council, the previous planning history, combined with the generous 0.22-acre site, may provide an attractive opportunity for purchasers considering redevelopment or an alternative future use of the property.

The current bungalow offers an excellent opportunity to enjoy a peaceful, detached home without the maintenance associated with a larger property, while the generous grounds provide significant scope for landscaping, extension or future development, subject to planning.

Delgany is one of County Wicklow's most desirable residential locations, renowned for its village atmosphere, mature surroundings and exceptional quality of life. The area combines the tranquillity of village living with excellent accessibility to Dublin and the surrounding towns. Delgany Village offers a selection of cafés, restaurants, shops and local amenities, while nearby Greystones provides an extensive range of schools, sporting facilities, restaurants, shops, the DART station, marina and seafront.

The surrounding area is particularly attractive for those who enjoy an outdoor lifestyle, with the Wicklow countryside, woodland walks, coastal paths and mountains all within easy reach. The N11 and M50 are also readily accessible, making the property an excellent choice for commuters seeking to combine a peaceful setting with convenient access to Dublin.

Whether purchased as a comfortable detached bungalow, a downsizing opportunity, an investment or as a property with potential for future development, No. 8 represents a rare and exciting opportunity on an exceptionally generous site in one of Wicklow's most sought-after locations.

Early viewing is highly recommended.

Planning Potential

The property has the benefit of previous planning history, having previously benefited from planning permission for a new detached dwelling under Planning Reference 23/60473 The combination of the generous 0.22-acre site and previous planning history makes this an interesting proposition for purchasers considering the property's longer-term potential.

Any future development would be subject to a new planning application, compliance with current planning policy and the requirements of Wicklow County Council. The previous planning permission should not be taken as confirmation that future planning permission will be granted, and prospective purchasers are advised to satisfy themselves fully in relation to all planning matters.

Location

Delgany is a highly sought-after village located in the heart of North Wicklow, offering an exceptional blend of countryside tranquillity, village charm and accessibility. The village and surrounding area are home to a wonderful selection of cafés, restaurants, shops and local amenities, while nearby Greystones provides an extensive range of primary and secondary schools, sporting facilities, the DART, marina and seafront. The location is particularly appealing for outdoor enthusiasts, with an abundance of scenic walking and cycling routes, woodland, countryside and coastal amenities all within easy reach.

For commuters, the N11 and M50 are readily accessible, providing excellent connectivity to Dublin City Centre and surrounding areas.
